# Unity — Brand Identity

## Mission
To connect people through the sharing of items — helping South Africans make money from what they own and save money by renting instead of buying.

## Brand Values
- **Solution-oriented** — Unity solves real problems (affordability, idle assets)
- **Versatile** — Works across categories: tech, tools, fashion, outdoor, vehicles, events
- **Safe** — Trust is the product. Every decision reinforces security.
- **Likeable** — Warm, human, community-driven
- **Pioneer** — First mover in SA's verified P2P rental space

## Brand Personality
Unity feels like a trusted friend who happens to know everyone in the city.
Confident but not arrogant. Warm but not soft. Smart but accessible.

## Tone of Voice
- Clear and direct — no jargon
- Empowering — "you can do this"
- Story-driven — real people, real moments
- Optimistic — solutions, not problems

### Examples
| Don't say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "Utilize our platform to facilitate transactions" | "Rent it out. Make money. Simple." |
| "Our verification process ensures compliance" | "Every user is verified. So you can trust who you're dealing with." |
| "Items are available for rental" | "Someone near you has exactly what you need." |

## Visual Direction
- **Feel:** Premium but accessible. Clean, modern, warm.
- **Photography:** Real people, real moments. High production quality.
  Show: convenience, happiness, satisfaction, community.
- **Color palette:** Warm neutrals + bold accent. Avoid cold/corporate blues.
  Consider: deep charcoal (#1a1a1a), warm white (#fafaf8), bold accent (e.g. amber/orange)
- **Typography:** Modern, clean sans-serif. Readable at small sizes (mobile-first).

## Key Associations
- Harmony, wholesomeness, solution, versatility, safety
- Community, empowerment, access, trust

## Campaign Themes (for content reference)
Emotional, story-driven ads that show Unity changing real moments:
- The Boots — boy gets soccer boots via Unity, honouring his late father
- The Heatwave — renting a fan changes everything in a brutal summer
- The Hustler — renting a bike becomes a business
- The Barista — fired woman rents a coffee machine, opens a pop-up
- Loadshedding Showdown — generator rental saves the big match
- Toy Story Reimagined — forgotten toys rented to new children
- DIY Dad — single dad rents tools to build his daughter a treehouse
